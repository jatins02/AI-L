tensorboard is a visualisation toolkit, developed by the tensorflow team

go to tensorflow.org/tensorboard/ to read the documentation, this documentation is for tensorflow
go to pytorch.org/docs/stable/tensorboard.html to read the pytorch documentation for the tensorboard module

you can:
- track and visualise metrics like model accuracy and loss
- visualise model graph
- view histograms of weights, biases or often tensor as they change over time
- project embeddings to a lower dimension
- displaying images, test and audio data
etc.
